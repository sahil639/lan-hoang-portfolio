(About)

Turning the ideas into real work. From producing the project to art directing and set design.

Lana is a creative producer who takes a campaign from first idea to final frame — writing the storyboards, building the sets, directing the shoot and shaping the edit.

Her work spans iTVCs, commercial product photography, performance video ads for Meta and food photography, with campaigns for DOKI DOKI reaching millions of views on Instagram.
