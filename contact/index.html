Frequently asked questions

What does a creative producer do?

A creative producer runs the project. Lana leads the crew, manages the schedule and controls the budget, while shaping the concept and visuals — so one person owns both the creative and the delivery.

Can you work within a fixed budget?

Yes. Fitting the shoot to the budget is part of the job — Lana plans locations, crew, cast and shoot days around what is available, and optimises the shooting so the budget goes on what ends up on screen.

What kind of projects do you take on?

iTVCs and short-form brand films, product and campaign photoshoots, and performance video ads for social — especially for food, beverage, beauty and wellness brands.

Can you work with my in-house team or agency?

Yes. Lana can lead a production end to end, or plug into an existing team as producer, art director or set designer.

Do you handle both video and photo?

Yes — many projects capture both on the same set, which keeps the campaign visually consistent and the budget efficient.

How does a project start?

Send a short note through the form with your product, goal and timing. Lana will reply to set up a call, then follow with a concept and production plan.
