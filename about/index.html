Lana HoangCreative Producer — Art Director

Lana blends a producer’s discipline with an art director’s eye to turn ideas into work people stop scrolling for.

She takes a campaign from first idea to final frame — writing the storyboards, building the sets, directing the shoot and shaping the edit. Her work for DOKI DOKI spans iTVCs, product photography and performance video ads, reaching more than 10.8 million views on Instagram.

Away from client work she styles and shoots her own food photography, and steps in front of the camera as a model — both of which make her better at directing what happens behind it.

Producing

Budgets, schedules, crews and call sheets — the unglamorous structure that lets a creative idea survive contact with a real shoot day. Lana keeps projects moving and every department pointed at the same goal.

Art direction

Colour, set, props, wardrobe and light. Lana builds a visual world for every product — then directs talent inside it so each frame feels spontaneous, but stays on brief.

Process

A — F

  1. A

    Brief

    Understanding the product, the audience and what success looks like.

  2. B

    Concept & script

    One honest moment and one line people remember.

  3. C

    Storyboard

    Every beat, frame and product moment drawn before it’s shot.

  4. D

    Set & casting

    Designing, sourcing and dressing the world the story lives in.

  5. E

    Shoot

    Directing talent and art directing each frame on the day.

  6. F

    Edit & deliver

    Cutting for the feed and delivering formats ready to perform.

On set The best part of the job is the day itself — when the storyboard, the set and the people finally meet in front of the camera.
