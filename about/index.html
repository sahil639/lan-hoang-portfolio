Lana blends a producer’s discipline with an art director’s eye to turn ideas into work people stop scrolling for.

She takes a campaign from first idea to final frame — writing the storyboards, building the sets, directing the shoot and shaping the edit. Her work for DOKI DOKI spans iTVCs, product photography and performance video ads, reaching more than 10.8 million views on Instagram.

ProducingArt direction

Budgets, schedules, crews and call sheets keep an idea alive on a real shoot day. Colour, set, props, wardrobe and light give it a world to live in. Away from client work, Lana styles and shoots her own food photography and steps in front of the camera as a model — both of which make her better at directing what happens behind it.

Every project moves through the same six steps, from understanding the product and the audience to cutting formats ready to perform on the feed. Each step is drawn, planned and checked before the camera rolls, so the shoot day is spent on craft.

On set

The best part of the job is the day itself — when the storyboard, the set and the people finally meet in front of the camera. Lana runs the floor as art director: checking light, styling product between takes and keeping every frame on brief.
