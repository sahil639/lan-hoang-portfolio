Lana is a producer first: she runs the project, leads the crew and controls the budget — with an art director’s eye for every frame.

She manages a production from first idea to final frame — building the budget, hiring and leading the crew, scheduling the shoot days and optimising the shooting so it fits the budget, then writing the storyboards, building the sets and shaping the edit. Her work for DOKI DOKI spans iTVCs, product photography and performance video ads, reaching more than 10.8 million views on Instagram.

ProducingArt direction

Budgets, schedules, crews and call sheets keep an idea alive on a real shoot day — and a well-planned shoot puts more of the budget on screen. Colour, set, props, wardrobe and light give it a world to live in. Away from client work, Lana styles and shoots her own food photography and steps in front of the camera as a model — both of which make her better at directing what happens behind it.

Process

  1. ABrief
  2. BBudget and plan
  3. CScript and storyboard
  1. DCrew, cast and set
  2. EShoot
  3. FEdit and deliver

Every project moves through the same six steps. The budget is set early and every later decision — crew, cast, locations, number of shoot days — is planned to fit it, so the shoot day is spent on craft, not on surprises.

On set

The best part of the job is the day itself — when the storyboard, the set and the people finally meet in front of the camera. Lana runs the floor as producer and art director: leading the crew, keeping the day on schedule, checking light and keeping every frame on brief.
