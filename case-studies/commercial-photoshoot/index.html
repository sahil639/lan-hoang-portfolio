About the project

Art directing the product photoshoot for DOKI DOKI — three product lines, three colour worlds and one bright, playful brand voice.

Art direction

Each line was given its own palette pulled straight from the packaging: warm peach for CC Shot, soft blush for Luna and candy pink for Lean Up. Talent, wardrobe and props sit inside that colour so the product always reads first.

CC Shot

Expressive, joyful poses — balancing the product, sipping, laughing mid-frame — give the brand images that feel spontaneous but stay on brief.

Luna

A softer, blush-toned world for the collagen range, built around warm light and easy smiles.

Lean Up

Candy pink and clean white keep the full Lean Up range bold, bright and instantly recognisable on shelf and on feed.

Behind the scenes

On set as art director — checking light, styling product between takes and keeping every frame on brief.
