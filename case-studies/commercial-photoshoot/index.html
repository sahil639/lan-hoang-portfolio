Art directing the product photoshoot for DOKI DOKI — three product lines, three colour worlds, one bright and playful brand voice.

About the project

Art direction

Each product line was given its own palette, pulled straight from the packaging: warm peach for the orange CC Shot, soft blush for Luna, and candy pink for Lean Up. Talent, wardrobe and props were styled to sit inside that colour so the product always reads first.

On set

Lana directed talent through a run of expressive, joyful poses — balancing the product, sipping, laughing mid-frame — to give the brand images that feel spontaneous but stay on brief.
