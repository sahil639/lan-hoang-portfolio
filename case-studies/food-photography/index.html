About the project

From styling food to creating the art direction for every photo — a personal study in colour, balance and a little bit of tension.

Concept

Two colours, nothing else. Glossy red produce — chillies, peppers, tomatoes — set against a washed, powdery blue, with cutlery and glass used as sculpture.

Styling

Each frame is a small balancing act: a pepper pierced by a knife, a chilli on a fork, a tomato perched on glass. Every prop, angle and shadow was styled by hand.
