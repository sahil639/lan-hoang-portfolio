About the project

Approach

Performance ads live or die in the first second. Each script opens on a relatable hook — a gym session, a skincare routine, an unboxing — and gets to the product fast, shot natively for the vertical feed.

Making

Lana wrote the concepts, filmed the product footage and cut every edit herself, building a library of variations across CC Shot, Lean Up and Luna Shot for the brand to test on Meta.
