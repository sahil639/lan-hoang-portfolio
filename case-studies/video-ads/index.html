A — 3 — Video Ads

About the project

Brainstorming the video scripts, filming the product footage and editing the video ads to run on Meta platforms.

Approach

Performance ads live or die in the first second. Each script opens on a relatable hook — a gym session, a skincare routine, an unboxing — and gets to the product fast, shot natively for the vertical feed.

Making

Lana wrote the concepts, filmed the product footage and cut every edit herself, building a library of variations across CC Shot, Lean Up and Luna Shot for the brand to test.
