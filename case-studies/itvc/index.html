Turning the ideas into real work. A series of short-form iTVCs for DOKI DOKI — from producing the project to art directing and set design.

About the project

Concept

Each spot is built around one honest moment — a late-night snack craving, a pizza night that ends in a food coma, a skincare routine with too many steps — and answers it with a single line the audience remembers: “Less bites. Same satisfaction.” “Enjoy food. Skip the guilt.”

Production

Lana produced each film end to end: writing the storyboards, building the sets — a retro diner, a living-room pizza party, an orange studio tablescape — casting and directing talent, and art directing every frame on the day.

Results

Released on Instagram, the spots reached more than 10.8 million views combined. The Luna Shot film alone passed 4.1 million.

Storyboard

Every spot is drawn before it is shot. The storyboards lock the beats, the framing and the product moment, so the shoot day is about craft — not guesswork.

Set design

Each product gets its own world: a retro diner in red and checkerboard, a lived-in living room for pizza night, and a warm orange studio tablescape — designed, sourced and dressed by Lana.
